Research method

Evidence before conclusion

The design separates observation from classification. It first asks whether a device is visible, then measures architecture attributes independently, and only then applies transparent derivation rules.

01

Gate the input

Reject images that do not clearly show a physical device instead of assigning architecture scores to arbitrary objects.

02

Measure attributes

Score mechanical, analog, digital, and software-control evidence independently against opposing descriptions.

03

Derive the label

Use explicit rules to derive Analog / Mechanical, Digital / Electronic, Software Controlled, Hybrid, or Indeterminate.

04

Evaluate failures

Measure the device gate, each attribute, the final label, abstention behavior, and difficult held-out device families separately.

Why independent scores matter

Absence should be allowed to look like absence.

A smartphone can have high digital and software evidence while mechanical evidence remains low. A stereo can show both digital and mechanical evidence. The scores no longer compete for a forced 100% total.
