# Device architecture labeling guide

## Analog / Mechanical

Use when the device's primary visible operation is direct physical or continuous analog behavior: gears, springs, levers, valves, needles, dials, film, or manual mechanisms. Small incidental electronics should not dominate the primary function.

## Digital / Electronic

Use when digital logic or electronic control is central, but there is no strong visual evidence that user-configurable software, networking, or an application ecosystem is the defining behavior.

## Software Controlled

Use when firmware, programmable menus, networking, applications, a general-purpose operating system, or embedded computing is central to how the device is configured or used.

## Hybrid

Use when meaningful mechanical or analog mechanisms and digital or software control are both central. Modern appliances, printers, stereos, and vehicle systems often belong here.

## Indeterminate

`Indeterminate` is an abstention result, not a training label. Use it when the image does not contain enough visible evidence, the photograph is poor, or multiple architectures remain similarly plausible.

## Review protocol

A reviewer should inspect the Commons source page and the visible image. When possible, verify manufacturer/model documentation. Change `review_status` to `reviewed`, add a concise `label_reason`, and keep every image of the same product model in one split.
